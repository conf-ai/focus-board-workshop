# Capstone Challenge 1: "Our Users Keep Saying The Tool Feels Disorganized"

## Business Problem

Your product team has collected user feedback over the past month, and a clear pattern emerged: **"The tool feels disorganized."** This complaint appears in 23 support tickets, 8 user interviews, and multiple feature request forms.

Leadership is concerned because:

- Users are abandoning projects after reaching 20-30 tasks
- New user onboarding feedback mentions "overwhelming" and "chaotic"
- Power users report spending too much time "finding what matters"
- Competitors are gaining traction by marketing their "clean, organized" experience

**The problem:** Nobody can articulate what "disorganized" actually means or what would fix it.

**Your mission:** You have 60 minutes to understand the real problem and deliver a solution that makes the tool feel organized.

## Impact

- **User Retention Risk**: 18% of users become inactive after their first project exceeds 25 tasks
- **Competitive Disadvantage**: Marketing can't counter competitor claims about "superior organization"
- **Support Overhead**: 15+ hours per week spent on "how do I organize my work" support tickets
- **Expansion Limitation**: Enterprise prospects cite "lack of structure" as blocker during trials
- **Team Morale**: Product team demoralized by vague negative feedback they can't act on

## Success Criteria (Observable Outcomes)

### Minimum Viable Success

- [ ] **Problem Understanding**: Clear articulation of what "disorganized" means in your specific context
- [ ] **Evidence-Based Decision**: Solution choice backed by actual codebase analysis, not assumptions
- [ ] **Working Implementation**: Functional change that addresses the identified root cause
- [ ] **Validation**: Ability to explain how the solution improves perceived organization

### Full Success

- [ ] **Systematic Discovery**: Used structured approach to explore multiple problem interpretations
- [ ] **Focused Solution**: Delivered one high-impact change rather than half-finished feature list
- [ ] **Context Evolution**: Documented organizational principles in AGENTS.md for future reference
- [ ] **Agent System Growth**: Extended agent system to handle problem discovery more effectively

## Real-World Context

This challenge mirrors situations senior engineers face constantly:

- Vague user feedback that needs interpretation
- Stakeholders who know something's wrong but can't articulate what
- Pressure to "just fix it" without understanding root cause
- Need to validate assumptions before committing resources

**The skill being tested: Can you use AI to think through ambiguity, or only to implement specifications?**

## Time Allocation Guidance

- **0-15 minutes**: Problem discovery and interpretation analysis
- **15-40 minutes**: Focused implementation of chosen solution
- **40-50 minutes**: Validation and testing
- **50-60 minutes**: Documentation and reflection
